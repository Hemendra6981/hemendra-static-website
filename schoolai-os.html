The Readiness Gap

Schools do not need AI hype. They need institutional readiness.

Most schools are not struggling with AI awareness alone. They are struggling with readiness.

The real gaps are teacher confidence, governance clarity, bilingual implementation, infrastructure adaptability, and structured benchmarking.

SchoolAI OS helps institutions understand where they stand before investing in tools, training, or technology deployment.
