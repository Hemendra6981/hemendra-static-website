Audit-First Strategy

Before schools adopt AI tools, they need readiness intelligence.

The SchoolAI assessment helps institutions understand current readiness before investing in AI platforms, training programs, or technology deployment.

It measures teacher confidence, governance clarity, infrastructure practicality, bilingual classroom needs, and implementation capacity.

What It Measures

10-domain institutional readiness model

Leadership & Vision Digital Infrastructure Teacher AI Readiness Academic Operations Administrative Operations Student Future Skills Governance & Compliance Government Alignment Parent & Market Readiness Change Management Capacity

Who Participates

Multi-stakeholder institutional assessment

Principal

Leadership vision, modernization priorities, governance maturity, and implementation readiness.

Teachers

Classroom AI confidence, productivity needs, bilingual teaching realities, and training readiness.

IT/Admin

Infrastructure, ERP/LMS systems, connectivity, devices, and implementation feasibility.

Students

AI literacy exposure, future-skills awareness, research capability, and responsible AI understanding.

Auditor

Evidence validation, benchmark review, scoring verification, and roadmap preparation.

Assessment Process

From registration to readiness report

01

Registration

Institution registers for assessment.

02

Assessment

Stakeholders complete assigned sections.

03

Validation

Auditor reviews evidence and responses.

04

Scoring

Weighted institutional readiness score generated.

05

Report

School receives benchmarked roadmap and recommendations.
